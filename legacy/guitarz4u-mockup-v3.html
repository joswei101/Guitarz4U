About Guitarz4U

A guitarist's approach to a fair deal

Guitarz4U was founded by Shua Weinfas, a self-taught pianist, guitarist and vocalist with a lifelong passion for music and the instruments that make it possible.

That hands-on experience shapes how every instrument is assessed and valued here — with an ear and eye for genuine quality, not just a price tag. Whether you're parting with an old favourite or looking for your next guitar, you're dealing directly with someone who understands what it means to the person playing it.

Buying & selling guide

A straightforward look at how each side of Guitarz4U works.

Buying from Guitarz4U

  1. Browse the shop — every guitar listed has been inspected and priced individually, and treated as a unique, one-off item.
  2. Add a guitar to your cart, or enquire by email or WhatsApp for further photos or details.
  3. Reserve it through checkout, then pay via EFT or cash and arrange collection.

Selling to Guitarz4U

  1. Submit your guitar's details through the sell/trade form, including honest notes on its condition.
  2. Receive a personal, no-obligation offer based on condition, market value and demand.
  3. Accept the offer and arrange handover — payment is made via EFT or cash.

Frequently asked questions

How is a guitar's condition graded?

Listings use four condition grades — Excellent, Very Good, Good and Fair — reflecting the instrument's playability and cosmetic state after inspection.

What does the inspection include?

Every guitar is checked for electronics, neck, frets, tuners, bridge, body, finish, pickups and general playability before listing.

How quickly will I receive an offer after submitting?

Every submission is reviewed personally by Shua. You'll receive a direct reply with an offer as promptly as possible.

How do payments work?

Guitarz4U accepts EFT (bank transfer) and cash. Card and online payment processing are not currently available.

Can I view a guitar in person before buying?

Yes — viewings can be arranged. Get in touch via WhatsApp or email to set up a time.

How does collection work?

Most guitars are collected in person once payment has been arranged. See the Shipping & Collection policy under Legal for full details.

Can I return a guitar?

See the Returns & Refunds policy under Legal — as second-hand items, returns are considered case-by-case and depend on accurate disclosure of condition at the time of listing.

What if I don't see my guitar type listed?

Inventory changes regularly. Use the contact details below to ask whether something similar is available or expected soon.

Returns & Refunds Policy
As second-hand instruments, guitars are sold based on the condition described and photographed at the time of listing. If an item received materially differs from its description, please contact us within 7 days of collection or delivery to discuss a resolution, which may include a repair, replacement or refund at Guitarz4U's discretion, consistent with your rights under the Consumer Protection Act. Change-of-mind returns are not automatically accepted for second-hand goods but may be considered case-by-case.
Shipping & Collection Policy
Collection in person is the preferred and default option, arranged directly after payment is confirmed. Courier delivery may be arranged on request at the buyer's cost and risk; Guitarz4U will package instruments carefully, but is not liable for damage occurring in transit once handed to a courier — any transit damage claim should be raised with the courier, and we will assist where reasonably possible.
Payment Information
Guitarz4U currently accepts EFT (bank transfer) and cash on collection. No online card payment or automated payment processing is currently offered through this website, and no card details are collected or stored on this site.
Warranty / Guarantee Policy
Every guitar is inspected prior to listing, covering electronics, neck, frets, tuners, bridge, body, finish, pickups and general playability, and is described honestly based on that inspection. Guitars are sold as used goods in the condition described; no manufacturer warranty is implied unless the instrument is still within an existing manufacturer warranty period.
Used-Item / Condition Policy
All instruments are pre-owned and may show signs of wear consistent with age and use. Condition is graded as Excellent, Very Good, Good or Fair, with any known defects or included accessories noted on the individual listing. Buyers are encouraged to review listing details carefully or arrange a viewing before purchase.
