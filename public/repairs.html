Repair FAQ

How often should I get a setup?

Once or twice a year for most players, and whenever you change string gauge or notice buzzing, high action or tuning problems. South Africa's seasonal humidity swings can move necks noticeably.

Do I need to book, or can I walk in?

Booking online means we can plan workshop time and you'll get a tracking reference. Walk-ins are welcome during workshop hours; we'll book you in on the spot.

Will you start work without my approval?

Never. We inspect the guitar, confirm a quote, and only start once you've agreed. If we find anything unexpected during the work, we'll check with you first.

How do I pay?

Payment is due on collection, by EFT or cash. For larger jobs like refrets or neck resets we may ask for a deposit to cover parts.

What happens if I can't collect right away?

No problem. We'll keep your guitar safe for up to 30 days after it's ready. After that, please get in touch so we can arrange something.
