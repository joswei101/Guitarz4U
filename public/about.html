A guitarist's approach to buying, selling and fixing guitars

Our story

Run by Shua Weinfas

Guitarz4U started from a simple frustration: buying a used guitar shouldn't feel like a gamble.

Every guitar in the shop has been played, inspected and set up in our own workshop before it's listed. We grade condition honestly, photograph the flaws as well as the flame tops, and price fairly for the South African market.

The same workshop looks after customers' guitars too. Setups, fretwork, nuts and saddles, crack repairs and more are done carefully, explained clearly, and priced up front.

Condition guide

How we grade guitars

MintAs new. No visible wear; may be unplayed or barely played.
ExcellentVery light signs of use, e.g. minor surface marks only visible up close. Frets near new.
Very GoodNormal light play wear: a few small dings or scratches, light fret wear. Plays perfectly.
GoodClearly used, with visible dings, finish wear or buckle rash. Fully functional and set up.
FairHeavy cosmetic wear or a known issue (described in the listing). Priced accordingly.
Policies

Buying & repair policies

Reservations & payment

Reserving a guitar online is free and holds it for 48 hours while we confirm the sale with you. Payment is by EFT (bank details are sent on confirmation, use your reservation reference) or cash on collection. Guitars are released once payment has cleared.

Collection & courier

Collect from us in Johannesburg by appointment, or we can arrange insured nationwide courier at cost. Guitars are packed in a hard case or a guitar box with extra padding and loosened strings.

Returns

If a guitar arrives with an issue that wasn't described in the listing, contact us within 7 days and we'll make it right with a repair, exchange or refund. Please inspect courier deliveries on arrival.

Repair quotes & approval

Prices listed are starting prices. We confirm a quote at drop-off and never start work without your approval. Parts (strings, fret wire, pickups, hardware) are charged at cost where noted.

Repair warranty

Our workmanship is guaranteed for 3 months. If something we've done isn't right, bring it back and we'll sort it at no charge. This doesn't cover new damage, wear or seasonal neck movement.

Uncollected guitars

We'll hold finished repairs for 30 days after notifying you they're ready. After that, please contact us to arrange collection.

Privacy (POPIA)

We only use the details you give us to handle your reservation, repair or enquiry, and never sell or share them with third parties except a courier when you request delivery. Ask us any time to see or delete your information.
