## Second-hand equipment, honestly described

Everything in the Guitarz4U shop is second-hand unless stated otherwise. Each item is inspected before listing and given a condition rating plus written condition notes. The photos are of the actual item.

## Condition ratings

- **Mint** — little or no visible wear; plays and functions as new.
- **Excellent** — light signs of use only; fully functional and well set up.
- **Very good** — normal cosmetic wear such as light scratches or buckle rash; fully functional.
- **Good** — noticeable wear and/or minor non-structural issues, all disclosed in the notes; plays well.
- **Fair** — heavy wear and/or functional issues disclosed in the notes; priced accordingly.
- **For repair / project** — sold as a project or for parts. Known faults are described; not guaranteed to be playable.

## What inspection covers

Where applicable we check the neck, frets, nut, tuners, bridge and saddle, electronics, pickups, switches, jack socket, body, finish and general playability. Amplifiers are checked for power-up, basic function and audible faults.

## Limitations

An inspection cannot reveal every possible issue, and older instruments and electronics can develop faults over time. Strings, setup preferences and minor cosmetic marks are not considered faults. Please ask questions or request a viewing before buying if anything matters to you. Your statutory rights are not affected.
