## A guitarist's approach to a fair deal

Guitarz4U is a small, owner-run business in Sandringham, Johannesburg that buys and sells second-hand guitars and equipment and repairs guitars and amplifiers.

It was started by a musician — a self-taught pianist, guitarist and vocalist — who has spent years playing, fixing and setting up instruments. That hands-on experience shapes how every instrument is assessed, repaired and priced: with an ear and eye for genuine quality, not just a price tag.

## What we do

- **Buy** used, broken and incomplete guitars, and selected guitar-related parts and equipment.
- **Sell** inspected second-hand guitars, amplifiers and selected parts, each described honestly with a condition rating and photos.
- **Repair** electric, bass, acoustic, classical and electro-acoustic guitars, guitar amplifiers and, subject to approval, other string instruments.

## How we work

- Every instrument for sale is a unique, individually inspected item. What you see in the photos is the actual item.
- Known faults and wear are disclosed in the listing's condition notes.
- Repairs are only carried out after an inspection and a quote that you approve. You pay after the work is done.
- Collection only, in the Sandringham area. The exact address is shared privately once a purchase or repair is confirmed.
- We are closed on Saturdays.
