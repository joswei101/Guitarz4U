## Your statutory rights

This policy does not limit your rights under South African law. In particular:

- The **Consumer Protection Act (CPA)** gives consumers rights regarding goods that are defective or not as described, including an implied warranty of quality. For second-hand goods, the item's age, disclosed condition and description are relevant.
