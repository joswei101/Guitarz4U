## Requests and acceptance

Submitting a repair request does not create a contract. The owner reviews every request and may decline jobs that cannot reasonably be completed. Drop-off arrangements are made privately.

## Inspection and quotes

Every job is inspected before a quote is prepared. The quote lists labour, parts, the total, warranty terms and exclusions. **No work begins until you explicitly accept the quote.** If further problems are found during the repair that would change the price, we will ask you before doing additional work.

## Limited repair warranty

Each completed repair carries a limited warranty covering the specific work listed on the accepted quote, for the duration stated on that quote. The warranty does not cover:

- accidental damage, misuse, or modification by anyone else after collection;
- damage from humidity, temperature changes or environmental conditions;
- normal wear such as string, fret or finish wear;
- parts supplied by the customer;
- unrelated faults not included in the quote.

To make a warranty claim, contact us with your repair reference. Your rights under the Consumer Protection Act (including section 57 on repair services) are not affected.
