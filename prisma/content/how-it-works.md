## Buying from Guitarz4U

1. **Browse the shop.** Every listing is a unique item with real photos, a condition rating and honest condition notes.
2. **Ask questions.** Use the questions section on any product page, or WhatsApp us for extra photos or a viewing.
3. **Create an account and verify your email.** An account is required for checkout so we can keep your order, receipt and collection details private.
4. **Check out with your card.** Items are held for you for a limited time while you pay securely through PayFast. Your order is only confirmed once the payment gateway verifies the payment.
5. **Arrange collection.** We contact you privately with the collection address and a suitable time in Sandringham, Johannesburg. Collection only — we do not offer courier delivery by default.

## Selling your guitar to Guitarz4U

1. **Submit your item** using the Sell Your Guitar form — include clear photos and describe any damage or faults. You do not need to name a price.
2. **We review it personally.** We may ask for more information or photos.
3. **Receive an offer** — or a polite decline if it isn't something we can buy. There is never any obligation to accept.
4. **Negotiate if you like.** Counter-offers are welcome and every step is recorded.
5. **Hand over and get paid.** Once a price is agreed, we arrange a private handover and payment.

## Getting a repair

1. **Request an assessment** with the repair form, including photos and a description of the problem. No payment is taken at this stage.
2. **We review the request** and let you know whether we can take the job on.
3. **Inspection and quote.** After inspecting the instrument, the owner prepares a written quote listing labour, any parts you will pay for, warranty terms and exclusions.
4. **You approve the quote.** Work only starts after you explicitly accept it — online in your account, or confirmed in writing.
5. **Repair and updates.** Track progress in your account or on the repair-tracking page.
6. **Pay and collect.** Payment is due only once the repair is complete. Then collect your instrument.
