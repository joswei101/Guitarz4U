## Submitting an item for purchase consideration

- Submitting an item does not oblige you to sell, or Guitarz4U to buy.
- We only consider instrument and equipment types listed on the form. Arbitrary parts are not accepted automatically.
- You do not need to name an asking price. If we are interested, we will make an offer, which you may accept, decline or counter.
- Please describe faults and damage honestly. Offers are based on the information and photos provided and may change after an in-person inspection.
- You confirm that you own the item and are entitled to sell it. We may ask for proof of ownership and we will not buy items we suspect are stolen. [OWNER TO CONFIRM — any second-hand goods record-keeping requirements that apply to your business, with legal advice.]
- Your photos and videos are stored privately and used only to assess your item. They are not published.
- Submissions never become public listings automatically. If we buy your item, we create our own listing with our own photos.
