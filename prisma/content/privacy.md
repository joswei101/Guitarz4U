## What we collect and why

We collect only what we need, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA):

- **Account details** (name, email, password stored only as a secure one-way hash, optional phone number) — to provide your account, orders and repair tracking.
- **Order information** (items, amounts, payment status, collection arrangements) — to sell and hand over goods, provide receipts and meet record-keeping obligations.
- **Payment information** — card payments are processed by PayFast. We never receive or store your full card number or security code. We store the payment reference and status returned by the gateway.
- **Sell and repair submissions** (contact details, instrument details, photos and videos you upload) — to assess your item or repair. These uploads are stored privately and are only visible to you and authorised staff.
- **Messages** you send us — to respond to you.
- **Newsletter email** — only if you opt in. You can unsubscribe at any time.
- **Basic website statistics** — we count page views by page address only. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking, and we do not store your IP address for analytics.

Photos you upload have location (GPS) and camera metadata removed when they are processed.
